What is ranked-choice voting?

RCV is a voting system that identifies the candidate with the most widespread support. In our modified RCV, even if your 1st choice doesn't win, your other choices (2nd, 3rd, 4th, or 5th) still count in determining the outcome.

How are votes counted in modified RCV?

If a candidate receives more than 50% of 1st-choice votes, they win. If no candidate meets that threshold, rounds of elimination begin. In each round, the candidate with the fewest votes is eliminated. If your highest-ranked candidate is eliminated, your vote moves to the next highest-ranked candidate. This continues until a candidate wins, or Round 5 is reached — at which point the candidate with the most votes in that round wins.

Can I vote for only one candidate? Will my ballot still count?

Yes, your ballot will count even if you vote for fewer than 5 candidates. Ranking additional candidates will not hurt your top choices. Your vote moves to your lower-ranked candidates only if your higher-ranked candidates are eliminated.

What if every candidate I ranked gets eliminated?

Your ballot becomes "exhausted" — it drops out of the count for the rest of the tallying. It doesn't transfer to anyone else and doesn't count against anyone either; it simply stops influencing the outcome from that round on.

Can I change my ballot after submitting?

Yes. Enter the same name and this form will load your previous ranking so you can adjust it. Submitting again replaces your earlier ballot — only your latest one counts.
